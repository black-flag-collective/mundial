# 🏆 Bracket — projected knockout

A full projected knockout bracket, **Round of 32 → Final**, laid out left and
right toward a center Champion card.

## How it's built

- Seeded from **group favourites**.
- Later-round winners are **projected by world ranking** until real results
  replace them.
- Knockout window: **Jun 28 – Jul 19**.

## What you can do

- **Hover any matchup** to highlight both nations on the globe.

As real knockout results come in through the data pipeline, projected matchups
give way to actual ones.
