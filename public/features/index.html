The five views
🔥
Live attention

Buzz

"Who the world is watching" — a leaderboard of the nations generating the most chatter right now, ranked by a live buzz score.

  • Live count & timestamp — refreshed every few seconds.
  • Each row: rank, flag, FIFA code, name, an intensity bar, and the score.
  • ▲ SURGING marks a sharp spike — a goal, a result, a viral moment.
  • 🔥 Trending on X — a strip of the hottest World-Cup trends, and a TRENDING badge on nations currently trending (when the live feed is on).
  • Tap a row to inspect what we're pulling from X for that nation — the query, post volume + sparkline, peak/now rate, live-match head-to-head, and its trending topics.
  • 🔮 Ask the Oracle for a data-grounded, shareable prediction of the champion + final.
  • Graceful states for loading, "all quiet," and a lost-signal retry.
🌍
48 nations · 3D

Globe

An interactive globe of Earth with every qualified nation marked, and the live buzz rippling across its surface.

  • Drag to spin; it idles with a gentle rotation on its own.
  • Hover a nation for its group, confederation, live buzz rank, and group-stage road.
  • A confederation-coloured wave field brightens where buzz concentrates.
  • The "Buzzing now" chip names the leader and cycles the top nations — click to open the board.

Day by day

Matchday

A timeline of the tournament, one day at a time, with live scores.

  • Date slider scrubs through every matchday; TODAY is highlighted automatically.
  • Featured match center-stage with score/status (Final, Live, or kickoff) and venue.
  • Fixtures slate lists the day's matches — click to feature, hover to trace on the globe.

Standings

Live group tables, computed directly from match results — never hand-entered.

  • All 12 groups, marking the top two advancing to the Round of 32.
  • Hover a team to trace it on the globe below.
  • A timestamp shows how fresh the underlying data is.

Bracket

A full projected knockout bracket, Round of 32 through the Final.

  • Seeded from group favourites; later winners projected by world ranking until real results replace them.
  • Hover a matchup to highlight both nations on the globe.
  • Knockout window: Jun 28 – Jul 19.
How Buzz and the Globe connect

The pulses you see are the live buzz

The buzz layer isn't a separate gimmick — the globe and the board are two views of one signal, wired both ways.

Globe → Board

The wave-field pulse, the spotlight's "Buzz rank · score," and the cycling "Buzzing now" chip all surface the same live signal. Tap to open the board.

Board → Globe

Click a leaderboard row and the Globe opens focused on that nation; the camera settles on it and its row scrolls back into view.

Surge toast

When a nation surges while you're on the Globe, a small ▲ "…is surging" toast slides in up top — so a spike reads across both views. Rate-limited, never spammy.

Reduced motion: if your system requests it, the chip stops cycling, the surge toast is suppressed, and scrolling jumps instead of animating. The data stays just as live.

How the site stays live
⚽

Match data

Scores, schedule, standings, and bracket refresh from a backend pipeline. As results land, Matchday updates and Standings & Bracket re-derive from those scores. Confirmed finals are never overwritten.

📡

Buzz feed

Polled every ~4 seconds. It can be driven by real activity from the X (Twitter) API — per-nation post volume mapped onto each country's score and spikes — or by a built-in simulated source. The on-screen experience is identical either way.

Privacy: the buzz layer only ever stores aggregate intensity per country. No post text, authors, or account IDs are kept or shown.
