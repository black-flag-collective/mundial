# 📅 Matchday — day-by-day results

A timeline of the tournament, one day at a time, with live scores.

## What you can do

- **Scrub the date slider** along the bottom to move through every matchday.
  The day marked **TODAY** is highlighted automatically based on the current
  date.
- **Featured match** sits center-stage for the selected day, showing the
  score/status — **Final**, **Live**, or the **kickoff time** — and the venue.
- **Fixtures slate** (right) lists every match that day:
  - **Click** one to feature it.
  - **Hover** a match to trace both nations on the globe behind.

## Live scores

Scores here update as the data pipeline ingests results. As they land, the
[Standings](standings.md) and [Bracket](bracket.md) re-derive themselves from
those scores. Confirmed final scores are never overwritten.
